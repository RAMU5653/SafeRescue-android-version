# Phase 9 — Skill Application

## Impeccable
- Clear risk hierarchy and readable score presentation.
- Avoided alarming language such as “crime detected”.

## Taste
- Risk information is concise, contextual and secondary to the manual SOS control.
- Empty/inactive states remain calm rather than showing fabricated intelligence.

## Motion
- Phase 9 does not add distracting animation. Existing controlled dashboard motion remains the interaction layer.

## Cybersecurity
- Deterministic local scoring minimizes data exposure.
- Explicit factor IDs make decisions auditable.
- Manual SOS is never dependent on risk output.
- No raw voice/location data is exported by the risk engine.
