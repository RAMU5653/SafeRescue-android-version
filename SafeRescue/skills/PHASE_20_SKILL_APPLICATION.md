# Phase 20 Skill Application

## Applied skills
- Impeccable: keep performance status logic invisible to the emergency UX; optimize without adding distracting controls.
- Taste: prefer one small policy object over many device-specific branches.
- Motion: do not animate or throttle the critical SOS countdown; optional updates may be less frequent under constrained conditions.
- Cybersecurity: battery/thermal state is local-only; optimization never disables encryption, TLS, emergency persistence/queueing, or evidence capture.
